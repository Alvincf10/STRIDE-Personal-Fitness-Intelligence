# DESIGN.md — STRIDE

## Product
STRIDE: personal fitness intelligence companion for one Garmin Forerunner 165 owner. Running-first, with recovery, coach, goals, and journey.

## Audience
A solo runner who wants clarity, progress, and honest guidance from their own Garmin data.

## Personality
Warm coach energy. Direct. Numbers stay precise; language stays human. Never medical. Never fake hype.

## Visual language
Gen Z Fitness × Premium Sports (directional only; original components).
Dark forest near-black green, electric lime accent, soft gray and muted green.
Large expressive metrics, rounded cards (18–24px), subtle lime wash, light motion.
Motif: soft lime glow on the primary metric of a screen only.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and body. Tabular figures for metrics.

## Content rules
Never invent metrics or recommendations. Unavailable / insufficient / stale stay explicit. Disclose calculation and rule versions on derived insights.
